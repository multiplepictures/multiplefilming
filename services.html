Our Production Services

Video Editing &
Post-Production

If you have collected a lot of raw footage or phone clips and aren't quite sure how to arrange them, we can help complete your video. We handle the basic assembly, pacing and color correction to get your videos ready to share.

Turnaround: 3-5 Business Days

Videography &
Camera Recording

Looking to capture a quick promo or local event? We bring a compact 4K camera and handheld stabilizer setup to get steady, clean footage on the move. Because we do not use a massive crew or heavy gear, we stay completely low-profile and out of the way while filming.

Turnaround: Dependent on project scale

Audio Mixing &
Sound Design

We help smooth out the sound elements of your project. We can help with basic voiceover, lowering distracting background room noise, balancing your volume levels and blending in sound effects with background music tracks so your videos flow smoothly.

Turnaround: 3-5 Business Days
